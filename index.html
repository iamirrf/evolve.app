Before you start

Is it free?

Yes. No credit card to create an account and start a path.

How long is a lesson?

About five minutes — one short lesson and a few adaptive questions. Keep going if you have the time.

Do I need a technical background?

No. Cognein is written for professionals in every field, not just engineers.

What powers the questions?

Gemini, adapting to the lesson you just finished and the areas you have found difficult.

Where can I use it?

On iPhone through the App Store, and in any modern browser. Android is on the way.

What happens to my data?

We do not sell your personal information. The detail is in the Privacy Policy, and you can delete your account any time.
