FAQ

Questions, answered

Is Cognein free?

Yes. Cognein is free to use — no credit card required to create an account and start a learning path.

Which devices can I use it on?

Cognein is available on iPhone through the App Store, and in any modern browser at app.cognein.com. An Android app is on the way.

How much time does it take each day?

A daily session is designed to take about five minutes — one short lesson plus a few adaptive questions. You can always keep going if you have the time.

Do I need a technical background?

No. Cognein is written for professionals in every field, not just engineers. Lessons start from what your job requires and explain the concepts as they come up.

What powers the assessments?

Gemini generates adaptive questions based on the lesson you just completed and the areas you have found difficult, then gives instant feedback on each answer.

Do I get a certificate?

Yes. Completing a course generates a verifiable certificate you can share with your employer or add to your LinkedIn profile.

How is my data handled?

We do not sell your personal information. Full detail on what we collect and why is in the Privacy Policy, and you can delete your account at any time from the app settings.
